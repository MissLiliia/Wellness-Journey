Exercise gallery & technique

Avoid 200 sit-ups or 200 squats every day. Start with controlled sets and take recovery days. If you feel nauseated, dizzy or dehydrated while adjusting to tirzepatide, reduce exercise and speak with your prescriber if symptoms persist. Core training does not selectively remove belly fat.
